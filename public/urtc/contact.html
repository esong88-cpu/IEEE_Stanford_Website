Is there a virtual option?

No. Stanford URTC is fully in person, for both presenters and attendees.

Can high school students present?

Yes. High school students with an undergraduate research affiliation, such as a college research program or faculty mentorship, can submit papers. All high school researchers can submit posters or lightning talks. See eligibility.

When does the call for research open?

We haven't set the dates yet. Join the interest list and we'll email you as soon as it opens.

Is this the same conference as MIT URTC?

No. Stanford URTC is inspired by MIT URTC and organized independently by Stanford students. It is not an official MIT event.
