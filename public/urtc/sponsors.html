§ 01 — What is URTC

Research, presented by students.

Stanford URTC brings together undergraduate researchers to present, discuss and advance engineering and technology research.

Students present papers, posters and lightning talks. Every accepted paper presented at the conference is published in IEEE Xplore. The day also includes a keynote, live judging, an audience vote and awards.
