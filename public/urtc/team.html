Join the TeamOrganizing team · 2027

Help build the first one.

Stanford URTC is run entirely by students. We're looking for four chairs to help launch the first conference in spring 2027.

§ 01 — Open roles

Four open chairs.

Each chair owns one part of the conference and works directly with the Conference Chair. You don't need to have organized a conference before. Curiosity and follow-through matter more.

  • Role 01Open

    Finance Chair

    Keeps the conference on budget and makes sure the money is handled properly.

    • Build and track the conference budget
    • Handle reimbursements and payments
    • Work with the Sponsorship Chair on funding
    Apply for Finance Chair →

  • Role 02Open

    Sponsorship Chair

    Finds and looks after the partners who fund the conference.

    • Build a list of prospective sponsors
    • Lead outreach and the sponsorship package
    • Be the main contact for sponsors before and on the day
    Apply for Sponsorship Chair →

  • Role 03Open

    Logistics Chair

    Plans everything that happens physically on the day.

    • Coordinate rooms, poster boards and AV
    • Plan check-in, catering and signage
    • Plan the optional Friday campus tours
    Apply for Logistics Chair →

  • Role 04Open

    Speakers and Program Chair

    Plans the schedule and the people on stage.

    • Invite and host the keynote speaker
    • Build the session schedule and run-of-show
    • Plan session moderators and the awards ceremony
    Apply for Speakers and Program Chair →

§ 02 — Process

How to apply.

  1. Apply

    Fill out the short application form and tell us which role, or roles, you're interested in.

  2. Talk

    The Conference Chair will follow up for a short conversation about the role.

  3. Build

    Join the team and start shaping the conference.
