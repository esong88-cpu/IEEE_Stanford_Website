Who can submit.

  1. Student work only

    Submissions must be the full and sole work of the student authors. Research must be completed before the authors receive their undergraduate degree.

  2. Faculty mentors

    Mentors who supervised the research may be listed as co-authors, but they can't be part of the presenting team.

§ 03 — Formats

Format requirements.

Every submission must align with one of the conference tracks. Tracks: TBD

  • Format A7 min

    Paper presentation

    • Manuscript of no more than 5 pages
    • Talk of no more than 7 minutes
    • Prepared slides that are clear, readable and professional
    • Published in IEEE Xplore once presented at the conference

  • Format B2 × 3 ft

    Poster

    • About 2 ft wide × 3 ft tall
    • Title at the top in capitals, about 1 in tall, with authors and affiliations below
    • Smallest text at least 3/8 in tall
    • Favor figures, diagrams and color over blocks of text, so the poster explains itself

  • Format C5 min

    Lightning talk

    • Write-up of no more than 4 pages
    • Talk of no more than 5 minutes
    • Covers the most essential parts of the research
    • Prepared slides that are clear, readable and professional

§ 04 — Review

Review & originality.

  1. Peer review

    Submissions are reviewed for originality and quality of content. Every presenter also receives written feedback from our technical chairs.

  2. Original, unpublished work

    Work must not be published, accepted for publication or under review elsewhere in substantially similar form. It must also be non-confidential and free of commercial content.

  3. No plagiarism

    Submissions are checked for plagiarism, including unattributed content and excessive reuse of earlier work. Violations are rejected, even after acceptance.

  4. Authorization to publish

    Authors must own the material they submit and get any consent needed to share it. IEEE copyright forms are required for all accepted papers.

§ 05 — At the conference

Presenting your work.

  1. One presentation per person

    Each author may present at most one submission across papers, posters and lightning talks.

  2. Who presents

    Only authors listed on the schedule may present. Not every author has to attend, as long as non-attending authors agree.

  3. Your content

    Presenters create their own presentations, based on the accepted submission. Updates and related new results are welcome.

  4. Present to publish

    Every accepted paper presented at the conference is published in IEEE Xplore. Papers that aren't presented won't be published.

  5. In person only

    All presentations happen in person at Stanford. If you can't attend, contact us as soon as possible.
