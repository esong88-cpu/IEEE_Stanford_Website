AboutStanford URTC 2027 · Frontiers

The undergraduate research conference.

Stanford URTC, the Stanford Undergraduate Research Technology Conference, is a one-day, student-run conference where students present engineering and technology research to peers, judges and the wider research community.

§ 01 — What is URTC

For undergraduates, by undergraduates.

Stanford URTC is a research conference for undergraduates, run by undergraduates. It's built around the engineering and technology research that students are doing right now, in labs, independent projects and classes.

Over one day, student researchers present their work, discuss it with peers, judges and invited experts, and get feedback that helps them take it further. The conference is organized by Stanford students with the Stanford IEEE Student Branch.

The conference is fully in person. There is no virtual attendance or presentation option.

§ 02 — Program at a glance

How the day runs.

The full schedule will be posted closer to the conference. Here's the shape of the day.

  1. Opening

    The conference chair welcomes presenters and attendees and introduces the year's theme, Frontiers.

  2. Paper presentations

    Presenters walk through their research papers in seven-minute talks, followed by questions from the audience. Every accepted paper presented at the conference is published in IEEE Xplore.

  3. Poster sessions

    The core of the day. Posters give a condensed snapshot of a project's results, and presenters talk through their work one-on-one with judges and attendees.

  4. Lightning talks

    Short, fast presentations of the most essential parts of a project.

  5. Keynote Speaker TBA

    A talk for the whole conference from a researcher working at the edge of their field.

  6. Judging, audience vote & awards

    Judges score presentations live and attendees vote for their favorites. The day closes with the awards ceremony.

  7. Written feedback

    After the conference, every presenter receives written feedback from our technical chairs.

§ 03 — Who it's for

Who comes to URTC.

We expect about 100 to 150 attendees.

  • 01Presenters

    Undergraduate researchers

    Undergraduates presenting engineering or technology research through a paper, poster or lightning talk.

  • 02Attendees

    Anyone curious

    Students thinking about research, mentors, faculty and people from industry who want to see what students are working on.

§ 04 — Lineage

A West Coast counterpart, in the spring.

Stanford URTC is inspired by MIT URTC, which the MIT IEEE Student Branch and IEEE Boston Section have run every October since 2015.

Our relationship with MIT URTC Stanford URTC is organized independently by Stanford students. It is inspired by MIT URTC and is not an official MIT event.

§ 05 — Organizers

Run by students.

Stanford URTC is organized by Stanford students with the Stanford IEEE Student Branch, with support from IEEE Boston Section.
